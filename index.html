Trotter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Trotter turns your walking into medals. It is made by one person, Eusha (contact: eushamashfi@gmail.com). This policy explains what Trotter collects, why, and the choices you have. Trotter has no ads and never sells your data.

What stays on your phone

  • Steps. Trotter reads your daily step count from your phone's own step counter (Android's Recording API or step sensor; Apple's Core Motion on iPhone). Steps are stored on your phone.
  • Approximate location. While the app is open, Trotter checks your approximate (city-level) location to work out which district, state or province you are in, so your steps can count toward that place's medals. The place lookup happens on your phone with a built-in map. Trotter never tracks your location in the background and never sends your coordinates anywhere; only the name of the place is kept.
  • Medals, your trophy case and settings.

You can use Trotter fully without an account. Without one, none of the above leaves your phone.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in with an email link or Google lets you back up your walking and appear in Ranks. We then store:

  • your email address (for signing in);
  • a private backup: steps per day, medals, your case, the names of places you've walked, monthly league results and settings, plus a random install ID;
  • for Ranks: your name as you set it (or "Anonymous walker" if you turn names off), your home area and country, and this month's step total. Ranks are public: anyone using Trotter can see the boards. They never show your email or location.
  • your wall: which medals and artifacts hang on it and where, so others can look at it from Ranks. It never includes your email or location, and anonymous walkers' rows can't be linked to their wall.

This data is stored with Supabase and protected so only your account can read or change your backup.

Analytics

Trotter uses PostHog to understand how the app is used, for example when a medal is earned, claimed or hung, or when the app is opened. These events carry a random ID and basic device information. They never include your location, coordinates or step history. You can turn analytics off any time in Profile → Settings.

Notifications

If you allow them, notifications are planned and shown on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to a server.

Purchases

Optional tips to support Trotter are handled by Google Play (or the App Store). Trotter never sees your card or payment details; it only receives confirmation of the purchase so it can give you a thank-you medal.

Trotter Pro

Trotter Pro (monthly or lifetime) is bought through Google Play (or the App Store). Trotter never sees your card or payment details; your phone only receives confirmation from the store that Pro is active. Pro belongs to your store account and is not stored on our server.

Invites

Each walker can share a personal invite link. When a friend installs Trotter from it, the friend's phone reads the invite code that Google Play passes along with the install (the "install referrer") and sends it to our server with a random install ID, so the person who invited them can be counted and rewarded. Each phone counts once. No name, contact or location is sent.

Artifact ideas

If you send an idea for a new artifact, we store the text you wrote, the app's language and a random install ID (to limit how many ideas one phone can send). Only the maker of Trotter can read them. Please don't include personal information.

Early Bird

The first walkers get an Early Bird medal. To hand these out, the app sends a random install ID to our server once. It contains no personal information.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade your data. It is shared only with the services named above (Supabase, PostHog, Google for sign-in, purchases and install referrals) to run the app, or if the law requires it.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your backup and your place in Ranks.
  • Without the app: email eushamashfi@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Trotter account". We delete it within 30 days.
  • On your phone: uninstalling Trotter removes everything stored on the phone.

Analytics events are kept for up to one year, then deleted.

Children

Trotter is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app when the change matters.
